# Product

This context records the approved specification and concepts 1/2 selected by the user. It does not reopen product discovery or replace the delivery checklist in spec.md.

## Register

product

## Users

People working across macOS apps throughout the day who need to recover recently copied text and reuse named addresses, signatures, and other snippets. They need quick keyboard access during another task and a larger library when organizing saved information.

## Product Purpose

Make local text retrieval quick and understandable while encrypting stored content. No account, server, telemetry, or network dependency. Community builds automatically open their login-Keychain-backed vault and capture new copies after OS approval. Pause/Disable choices persist; sleep purges state and wake resumes. Hide and pause is explicit presentation privacy, while Sensitive snippet actions retain fresh system authentication. Earlier protected builds remain separate. Windows is a later effort.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, familiar. The interface should feel like a focused native macOS utility. Use direct copy and explicit states; never imply security or capture behavior that has not been established.

## Anti-references

Decorative dashboards, card grids, neon accents, ornamental glass, oversized headings, and cloud/account-first onboarding. Concept 3 is unselected. A widget is a generic launcher, not a place to display saved text.

## Design Principles

- Return users to their task quickly: search, select, copy, dismiss.
- Keep behavior honest: Locked, Paused, permission errors, and developer previews are explicit.
- Make native controls familiar and preserve keyboard/VoiceOver use.
- Preserve the selected full-window and Focus-panel structures.
- Keep content local and sensitive bodies concealed until an explicit authenticated action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-only workflows, VoiceOver, increased contrast, reduced motion, resizable windows, and native light/dark appearance are requirements. Never rely on color alone or expose masked bodies through accessibility text. Detailed validation remains tracked separately in spec.md.
